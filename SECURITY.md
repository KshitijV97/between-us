# Security Policy

Design background: [docs/08-security-privacy.md](docs/08-security-privacy.md).

## Supported versions

The latest minor release receives security fixes. Older releases: best effort.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not open a public issue**. Use GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** (private security advisory)
on this repository. Include steps to reproduce, impact, and the affected version or commit
(`chatanalyzer version`).

**Never include real chat data.** Use synthetic examples with fictional participants.

## Our commitment

- Acknowledge within 3 business days.
- Triage and assign a severity (CVSS 4.0) within 7 days.
- Fix or mitigation target: critical 7 days, high 30 days, others in the next release.
- Coordinated disclosure; credit given unless you prefer otherwise.

## Scope

In scope: this repository's code, default configurations and deployment templates.
Out of scope: third-party services (report to them), denial of service against free-tier hosted demos,
social engineering.

## Safe harbour

Good-faith research that follows this policy will not be pursued legally.
